Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password? ▼

You can reset your password by clicking the "Forgot Password?" link on the login page, or visit the IT Help Desk in the Library basement with your student ID.

How do I register for classes? ▼

Class registration opens according to your academic standing. Check your registration date in the student portal, meet with your advisor, and then register online through the Course Registration system.

Where can I find my class schedule? ▼

Your class schedule is available in the student portal under "Academics" → "My Schedule". You can also view it in the mobile app or print a copy.

How do I pay my tuition bill? ▼

You can pay online through the student portal, by phone at (555) 123-PAY, by mail, or in person at the Bursar's Office. Payment plans are also available.

Who do I contact for technical support? ▼

For technical issues with the student portal or other IT services, contact the IT Help Desk at (555) 123-HELP or visit them in the Library basement, Room B10.

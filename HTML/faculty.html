Current Openings

We are always seeking exceptional faculty members to join our community. Current openings include:

  • Assistant Professor - Computer Science (AI/ML)
  • Associate Professor - Biomedical Engineering
  • Professor - Business Strategy
  • Lecturer - Mathematics

Application Process

  1. Submit Application: Include CV, cover letter, research statement, and teaching philosophy
  2. Initial Review: Applications reviewed by search committee
  3. Phone/Video Interview: Shortlisted candidates interviewed
  4. Campus Visit: Final candidates invited for on-campus interviews
  5. Job Talk: Present research and teaching demonstration
  6. Decision: Final selection and offer extended

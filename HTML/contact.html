Frequently Asked Questions

What are your campus tour hours? ▼

Campus tours are offered Monday through Friday at 10:00 AM and 2:00 PM, and Saturdays at 11:00 AM and 1:00 PM. Tours last approximately 90 minutes and include visits to academic buildings, residence halls, and recreational facilities.

How can I schedule a meeting with an advisor? ▼

You can schedule an appointment with an academic advisor through the student portal, by calling the Advising Center at (555) 123-4567, or by visiting the Academic Affairs office in person during business hours.

Where can I find parking on campus? ▼

Visitor parking is available in Lot C near the main entrance. Daily permits can be purchased at the parking kiosks for $5. Students and staff have designated parking areas with annual permits available through the Parking Services office.

What dining options are available on campus? ▼

We have three main dining halls, five cafés, a food court in the student union, and several grab-and-go locations. Dining options include international cuisine, vegetarian/vegan options, and accommodations for dietary restrictions.

How do I apply for financial aid? ▼

Start by completing the FAFSA (Free Application for Federal Student Aid) online. The priority deadline is March 1st. Contact our Financial Aid office at (555) 123-4569 for assistance with the application process and information about scholarships and grants.

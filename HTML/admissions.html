Admission Requirements

🎓 Undergraduate Admissions

Academic Requirements:

  • High school diploma or equivalent
  • Minimum GPA of 3.0 (4.0 scale)
  • SAT score of 1200+ or ACT score of 26+
  • 4 years English, 3 years Math, 3 years Science
  • 2 years Social Studies, 2 years Foreign Language

Required Documents:

  • Completed application form
  • Official high school transcripts
  • SAT or ACT scores
  • Letter of recommendation
  • Personal essay (500 words)
  • Application fee ($50)

Application Deadline: January 15 (Fall), October 1 (Spring)

🎓 Graduate Admissions

Academic Requirements:

  • Bachelor's degree from accredited institution
  • Minimum GPA of 3.0 in undergraduate work
  • GRE/GMAT scores (program dependent)
  • Prerequisites for specific programs
  • English proficiency (international students)

Required Documents:

  • Completed graduate application
  • Official transcripts from all colleges
  • 3 letters of recommendation
  • Statement of purpose (1000 words)
  • Resume/CV
  • Application fee ($75)

Application Deadline: December 1 (Fall), September 1 (Spring)

Tuition & Financial Aid

💰 Tuition & Fees (2024-2025)

Undergraduate (per year):

  • In-State: $15,000
  • Out-of-State: $28,000
  • Room & Board: $12,000
  • Books & Supplies: $1,500
  • Personal Expenses: $2,500

Graduate (per year):

  • In-State: $18,000
  • Out-of-State: $32,000
  • Professional Programs: $35,000 - $45,000

🎯 Financial Aid

We're committed to making education affordable. Over 85% of our students receive some form of financial assistance.

Types of Aid:

  • Merit Scholarships: $2,000 - $15,000
  • Need-based Grants: Up to full tuition
  • Federal Pell Grant: Up to $7,395
  • Work-Study Programs: $2,000 - $4,000
  • Student Loans: Federal and private options

Important Dates:

  • FAFSA Priority Deadline: March 1
  • Scholarship Applications: December 15
  • Aid Notification: April 1

Review Process

🌍 International Students

Additional Requirements:

  • English Proficiency:
    • TOEFL iBT: 80+ (Undergraduate), 90+ (Graduate)
    • IELTS: 6.5+ (Undergraduate), 7.0+ (Graduate)
    • Duolingo: 110+ (Undergraduate), 120+ (Graduate)
  • Financial Documentation (I-20 form)
  • Passport copy
  • Credential evaluation (WES recommended)

Support Services:

  • International Student Orientation
  • Academic English Support
  • Cultural Adjustment Programs
  • Immigration Advising
  • International Student Association

Estimated Costs (International Students):

  • Tuition: $32,000/year
  • Room & Board: $12,000/year
  • Health Insurance: $2,500/year
  • Personal Expenses: $3,000/year
  • Total: ~$49,500/year

Scholarships Available:

  • International Merit Scholarship ($5,000-$10,000)
  • Global Leadership Award ($15,000)
  • Regional Scholarships (varies by country)
  • Graduate Assistantships
